Services

Three ways I can help

Whether you're mid-remodel or about to list, the goal is the same: a home where every finish looks like it belongs.

Remodel design

Kitchens, bathrooms and whole-home interiors. I select tile, countertops, cabinet color and hardware, plumbing finishes and light fixtures so it all comes together as one cohesive look.

  • Finish and fixture selections
  • Lighting plan and fixture sourcing
  • Selections list ready for your contractor

Paint consultations

Choosing paint is harder than it looks. I'll walk your home, study the light in each room, and give you a whole-house color plan for walls, trim, cabinets, doors and exteriors.

  • In-home color walkthrough
  • Interior and exterior palettes
  • Exact paint names and sheens

Ready-to-sell refresh

Getting ready to list? Updated light fixtures and fresh, neutral paint are some of the most affordable ways to help a home show well. I'll pick what to change and what to leave alone.

  • Light fixture changeout picks
  • Buyer-friendly paint colors
  • A focused list that fits your timeline

About

Hi, I'm Elizabeth.

I believe a beautiful home comes from lots of small decisions that agree with each other: the warmth of a wood vanity next to brass sconces, a white kitchen that feels soft instead of stark, a front door color that welcomes you home.

My style leans warm, classic and transitional. I love painted shaker cabinetry, natural wood, aged brass and handmade-looking tile. Most of all, I love helping clients feel confident about choices they'll live with for years.

I work with homeowners across the North Atlanta suburbs, including Milton, Alpharetta, Roswell and Canton.
